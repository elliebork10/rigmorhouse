Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about hosting your event at Rigmor House.

What is your capacity?

Rigmor House can comfortably accommodate up to 225 guests for indoor events.

How much does it cost to host an event?

Rates vary based on the season and day of the week. Please contact us for a specific quote for your date.

Do you provide any tables or chairs?

Yes! We provide 43 rectangle tables, 90 conference chairs, and 25 folding chairs with your rental.

Do you provide caterers?

We have an open catering policy, allowing you to bring in any licensed caterer of your choice. We provide a full kitchen for their use.

Can we have access before/after our event?

We offer flexible rental periods. Please let us know your needs, and we will do our best to accommodate setup and cleanup time.
