Who We Are

Rigmor House is more than just a venue; it is a sanctuary for connection. Ideal for weddings, retreats, and celebrations, we comfortably accommodate up to 225 guests. Located in the tranquility of Chapel Hill, we offer a unique blend of privacy, modern amenities, and peace of mind for your most important days.

Ben Bork

As the manager of Rigmor House, Ben ensures that every event runs with precision and care. Dedicated to maintaining the legacy of the property, he works directly with clients to turn their vision into reality, whether it's a corporate workshop or a joyous wedding celebration.

Judi Loberg

The visionary behind Rigmor House, Judi’s inspiration grew from her years as a teacher and therapist. She dreamed of a private space away from the noise of city life—a place built with teaching in mind that could also embrace the beauty of a wedding day. Rigmor House is the fulfillment of that dream.

The Legacy of Rigmor

In Danish, Rigmor means "Rich Mother." Rigmor Loberg lived that name through her generosity, her courageous spirit, and her unwavering faith in others. She believed that work could be fun if done together and that everyone has something to offer. We named this house in her honor—a place to reconnect, refocus, and rejuvenate.

"She believed in me." — Judi Loberg
